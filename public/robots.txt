# /dashboard, /settings and /my-agents are signed-in surfaces and every one of
# their pages carries <meta name="robots" content="noindex">. A Disallow here
# would hide that tag from the crawler that needs to read it: Google kept them
# in the index as bare URLs ("Indexed, though blocked by robots.txt") precisely
# because it was never allowed to fetch the page and see the noindex. Crawling
# is what removes them, so leave these open.

# Embed routes serve raw runtime, not human content; keep them out of SERPs.

# Cosmetic OG image endpoints: fine for social crawlers, useless for SERP.
