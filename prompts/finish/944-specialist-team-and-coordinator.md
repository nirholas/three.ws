The idea to match: a private team of specialist bots working together, driven from one chat. A Researcher vets tokens, an Entry specialist watches for setups, a Trader executes inside the owner's rules and reuses the research, a Launcher prepares launches, and a coordinator routes plain-language requests to them, keeps work moving, and comes back to the owner only when approval or attention is needed. Several setups run at once.

We have the parts but not the product: delegation and hiring (api/agent-delegate.js, api/agent-collab.js, api/swarms), autopilot's propose, review, execute, undo loop (api/autopilot), the sniper worker and strategy compiler (workers/agent-sniper, api/sniper/compile.js), the safety and coin-intel endpoints, and the confirm-before-execute intents used by the Trading Copilot (api/_lib/wallet-intents.js, docs/trading-copilot.md). The existing concierge (api/concierge.js) only answers visitor questions.

Build teams as a first-class object: a team has a name, a shared spend policy, and role members that are real three.ws agents with their own bodies and pages. Build a shared findings board so the Trader cites a non-expired Researcher finding instead of redoing the work, and the report shows which finding justified which trade (reuse api/_lib/trade-receipt.js). Build the coordinator chat: it turns a sentence like "research this, watch for an entry under 50k market cap, buy 0.5 SOL if it passes" into a visible plan of role-tagged steps, streams step status, and turns every signing step into an approval card that pauses the plan until the owner decides. The coordinator remembers preferences (default size, risk, venues) through the agent-memory package and shows what it remembered.

Roles cannot exceed their permissions: a Researcher can never sign. Token names, descriptions and social text are untrusted data and must never steer the plan; add tests with hostile-looking real token metadata.
