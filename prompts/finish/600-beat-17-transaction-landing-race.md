## Why this matters

Lane D trading. Speed-sensitive trading agents win by landing transactions through several providers concurrently. The open execution toolkit races a dozen providers. `execution-engine.js` supports one Jito bundle route plus a plain RPC fallback.

## Tasks

1. **Provider list as data.** `landing_providers` config: name, submit endpoint, tip rule, auth env var. Providers that need a key we do not hold are listed disabled with the env var name; the Helius sender and plain RPC need no new vendor.
2. **Race.** `submitProtected` sends the same signed transaction to every enabled provider concurrently, resolves on the first confirmation, and treats duplicates as one (same signature).
3. **Stats.** Record per-provider landed/lost and latency in a table; the race orders by recent success and a provider with a failure streak is skipped until a probe passes (the lane-health pattern).
4. **Tip budget.** A per-trade tip ceiling honored by every provider.
5. Tests with local fake RPC servers that speak real JSON-RPC (not mocks of our own module).
