Wave at your laptop

No camera. The speakers play a tone you cannot hear, your hand bends it on the way back, and the microphone reads the shift. Left: the built-in response. Right: the same gestures, taken over by this page.

Use the built-in speakers and microphone and keep the volume up, because headphones break the reflection path. Hold still for the first couple of seconds while the room is measured. The carrier sits near 20 kHz, which cats and dogs can hear.

1. The built-in response

<agent-3d sonar> plus a button. A sweep steps the agent through its gesture vocabulary, a push moves the camera in and a pull eases it back out, a held lift turns the camera.
