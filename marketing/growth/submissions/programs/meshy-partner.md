## Fit warning (read first)

The program is built for **resellers and VARs with an existing customer base, agencies, consultancies and system integrators** selling **Meshy Enterprise** (SAML SSO, API console, custom solutions, SLA). It starts in Europe. Self-serve plans are excluded. "API or product-embedding interest can be raised on the intro call." three.ws wants embedded API pricing, which is not either listed partner type. The prospects table already flagged this ("aimed at enterprise resale"). Judgment call: submit anyway, because the form is two minutes, there is no fee, being a customer is not required, and the intro call is where embedding is discussed. State the real goal in the free-text field so nobody is misled.

## What it grants

Reseller: Enterprise licences at partner pricing with margin set in the agreement. Sales agent: commission on a first contract. Both: a named contact, enablement kit in English and French, co-marketing (webinars, case studies, launch support). Process: apply, reply within 2 business days to book a 30-minute call, then agreement.

## Deadline and eligibility

No deadline. No membership fee. Applicants state where they sell; the call confirms fit and coverage.
