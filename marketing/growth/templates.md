# Co-marketing dispatch templates

These templates use current three.ws facts and active opportunities. Re-verify the linked
proof immediately before sending. Relationship wording and logo use still follow the
partner-specific kits.

## The one-page partner brief

Every partner receives the same six-part brief, tailored to its audience:

| Part         | Required content                                                                     |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Outcome      | One sentence describing what the user can now do                                     |
| Partner role | The exact production technology or program involved                                  |
| Proof        | One public URL that works without private access                                     |
| Evidence     | Three current metrics or test results with capture dates                             |
| Assets       | One 16:9 image, one square image, one 15 to 30 second clip, exact alt text           |
| Ask          | One action: comment, repost, speaker, listing, newsletter, or editorial introduction |

Send the brief at least 48 hours before a social post and at least ten business days before
anything requiring brand review.
