## Wording rules for anything not on this page

- "Available in the IBM watsonx Orchestrate Agent Catalog" is accurate once live.
  "Certified by IBM", "IBM-approved", and "in partnership with IBM" are not. BYOL listings
  publish without IBM approval, so an approval claim is false on its face.
- Lead with what the customer can now do, not with the logo they will recognise.
- Never attach an IBM mark. See [`badge-usage.md`](badge-usage.md).
- The LinkedIn post carries the independence line because it names the partnership. Any
  post that names the partnership carries it too.
