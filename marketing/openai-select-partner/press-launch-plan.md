## How the IBM pickup happened (repeat it)

The IBM release went out on a newswire (GlobeNewswire, mirrored by AI Magazine) and
Business Insider Markets, Yahoo Finance and others syndicated it from the wire feed.
Business Insider does not accept direct pitches for this; the wire is the route.
Evidence trail: [`docs/ibm.md`](../../docs/ibm.md#press--coverage),
[`docs/listings-registry.md`](../../docs/listings-registry.md).

## Hard rules for the release

- Say "OpenAI Select Partner" exactly. No "partnered with OpenAI on <product>", no endorsement language.
- Keep the independence and trademark line at the foot.
- No token, price or crypto-performance language. The IBM coverage drew HTX token
  headlines; do not invite a repeat. $THREE does not appear in this release.
- Use the announcement card and badge only as supplied ([`badge-usage.md`](badge-usage.md)).
