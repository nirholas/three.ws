## Pitch it the right way

- **Lead with the enterprise and developer product.** 3D generation, MCP tools, the embeddable agent, the Microsoft integrations. These are what a Microsoft channel can retell.
- **$THREE and the Solana agent economy are not hidden, but they are not the pitch.** Microsoft's marketplace and brand teams handle cryptocurrency content conservatively. A pitch that opens with a coin loses the reader before the TRELLIS.2 or MCP story lands. If asked, say plainly that agent payments run on Solana through x402 and that $THREE is the platform's coin.
- **Claim only what is true today.** No partner tier, certification, co-sell status or endorsement. The banned phrasings for public copy are the `forbidden` list in [`data/x-content/partners/microsoft.json`](../data/x-content/partners/microsoft.json); the drafts file is linted against it. Private outreach to Microsoft staff may describe the Teams, Copilot and Power Platform packages as "built, awaiting submission", which is accurate. Public copy may not describe them as available.
- **Never say three.ws runs on Azure.** Production is on Google Cloud.
