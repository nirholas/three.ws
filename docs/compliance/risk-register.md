# Information security risk register

Part of the [information security policy](information-security-policy.md). Impact and likelihood are 1 (low) to 3 (high); score is the product. Owner of every row: the platform owner. Last reviewed: 2026-11-16. Next review due: 2027-11-16.

| # | Component | Threat | Existing control | Impact | Likelihood | Score | Treatment | Due |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Source repository | A change reaches production without a second human reviewer | Commit-message lint and `check:rules` at push, `db:check` and deploy audits before submit, owner-approved deploys, immutable Cloud Run revisions for rollback | 3 | 2 | 6 | Mitigate: owner decides on branch protection with required review; until then accept with these compensating controls | 2027-01-31 |
| R2 | Developer workstation | `.env.local` carries the production Neon `DATABASE_URL`, so development can touch production data | Tests and live checks use an in-process PGlite ([scripts/lib/isolated-db.mjs](../../scripts/lib/isolated-db.mjs)); Neon point-in-time restore | 3 | 2 | 6 | Mitigate: create a dedicated Neon development branch and switch `.env.local` to it | 2027-01-31 |
| R3 | Google Cloud | Long-lived user-managed service account keys (5 found 2026-11-16, two on the runtime account, three on a legacy `vercel-inference` account) | Keys are not in the repository (secret scan); least-privilege roles on the accounts | 3 | 2 | 6 | Mitigate: identify consumers, move them to workload identity or Secret Manager rotation, delete unused keys | 2026-12-31 |
| R4 | Production database | Loss or corruption of the Neon project | Neon point-in-time history, daily verified dump to a locked GCS bucket, measured RTO 215 s, RPO 24 h for the dump lane | 3 | 1 | 3 | Accept with controls; restore test runs on every backup | continuous |
| R5 | Dependencies | A vulnerable transitive package (`bigint-buffer` through `@solana/spl-token`, no upstream fix) | Vendored patched copy under `multiplayer/vendor/bigint-buffer`; daily Dependabot; `npm audit` evidence each run; triage table in [docs/security.md](../security.md) | 2 | 2 | 4 | Accept with recorded reason; revisit when `spl-token` ships a fix | 2027-02-28 |
| R6 | Web application | Vulnerability not found by internal review (no third-party penetration test yet) | Internal source-level review October 2026, security header regression check, automated dependency audit | 3 | 2 | 6 | Mitigate: commission an independent test (owner item) | 2027-02-28 |
| R7 | Sign-in | Account takeover | Opt-in TOTP for users, enforced second factor and 60-minute idle timeout for admin sessions, per-IP and per-account rate limits, hashed credentials | 3 | 2 | 6 | Accept; passkeys not offered (stated as not held) | 2027-06-30 |
| R8 | Custodial wallets | Signer key exposure | AES-256-GCM key encryption, leak scan every 15 minutes, critical alerts, wallet freeze procedure in the incident plan | 3 | 1 | 3 | Accept with controls | continuous |
| R9 | Third-party services | A subprocessor outage or breach | Published subprocessor list, provider failover ladders for AI lanes, status page | 2 | 2 | 4 | Accept; review on every provider change | continuous |
| R10 | Operations | Single person holds every role | Everything is scripted and documented, deploy and recovery runbooks, alerts to the owner by two channels | 3 | 2 | 6 | Mitigate: name a deputy with break-glass access when one exists | 2027-03-31 |
