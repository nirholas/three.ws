# Marketplace Rewards (now the Grow stage of Frontier Accelerate for Marketplace)

**Status:** not eligible yet; prepared so the owner can activate on the day the SaaS offer goes live. Program facts were read on 2026-11-12 from Microsoft's pages linked below.

## Current name

Marketplace Rewards has been folded into **Frontier Accelerate for Marketplace**:

- `microsoft.com/.../offers-benefits/marketplace-rewards` now answers with a 301 redirect to `partner.microsoft.com/en-us/partnership/frontier-accelerate-for-marketplace` (checked 2026-11-12).
- The Microsoft Learn page that described Marketplace Rewards now redirects to [Frontier Accelerate for Marketplace benefits](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/frontier-accelerate-marketplace/benefits) (page dated 2026-09-16).

The go-to-market benefits that used to be called Marketplace Rewards are the **Grow** stage of that journey: "Marketplace go-to-market benefits (Grow)", available on both the no-cost and Premium paths "as milestones are met".

## What qualifies

From the [Frontier Accelerate for Marketplace overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/frontier-accelerate-marketplace/overview):

- **Build and Publish** ends with "publishing a Marketplace-ready, transactable" listing.
- **Grow** is "publishing on Marketplace to driving Marketplace transactions".
- Benefits "unlock automatically based on meeting criteria for stages and tiers", and "Unused benefits don't carry between terms".

The ISV Success FAQ says the same: a contact-me listing satisfies Build and Publish, but "publishing transactable allows you to access Marketplace Rewards" ([ISV Success FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/isv-success#frequently-asked-questions)).
