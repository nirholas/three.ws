# What is trending on GitHub, 2026-11-10

A one-week follow-up to the [2026-11-03 benchmark](README.md). It looks only at what github.com/trending shows today and compares it with our top 40 repos.

| Group | Repos | How it was picked |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | 12 | github.com/trending, all languages, today |
| Weekly | 14 | Same, this week |
| Monthly | 14 | Same, this month |
| All three overall lists | 33 | Unique repos across daily, weekly and monthly |
| Every language list | 855 | The overall lists plus 30 per-language lists, each for daily, weekly and monthly |
| nirholas top 40 | 40 | Our 40 public repos with the most stars |

895 repos were fetched through the GitHub GraphQL API, with no misses. Forks are left out. Each repo has the same ~70 signals as the 2026-11-03 scan, plus the README's first screen (the first 40 non-blank lines), topic names, social preview, the last 8 release dates and the root file list. Every table is in the [appendix](#appendix-full-tables).

The overall lists are small (33 repos), so treat a gap under about 15 points as noise. The language lists are mostly older, slower repos (median age 4 years). They show the long tail, not the breakouts.

## The answer in six lines

1. **The trending page is now mostly coding-agent skills and plugins.** 14 of the 33 overall-list repos (42%) fall in that category, and so do 8 of the 14 monthly repos. MCP servers are 9%. Our top 40 are 58% MCP servers, so the wave has moved on from where most of our repos sit.
2. **Packaging is the visible marker.** 61% of overall-list repos have a `skills/`, `plugins/` or `.claude-plugin/` folder, 42% ship a `.claude-plugin/` marketplace manifest, and 61% have a config folder for a specific agent host. Our top 40: 12%, 5% and 10%.
3. **They release constantly.** 79% publish GitHub releases, 76% released in the last 30 days, and the median gap between recent releases is 2 days. Our top 40: 8%, 2% and 11 days on the few that release.
4. **They are busy in public.** The median overall-list repo has 354 commits to main in the last 30 days and 912 merged PRs. Ours: 8 and 4.
5. **Nothing trending is new.** None of the 895 repos is younger than 60 days. The newest was created 2026-09-09, and the median overall-list repo is 290 days old. Repos trend after months of shipping, not at launch. Our top 40 has a median age of 300 days, so age is not what holds them back.
6. **Their READMEs are a third the length of ours.** 255 lines and 15 headings against our 775 and 71. Their descriptions are 73 characters against our 214, with 3 topics against our 18.

The winning pattern is short. Look at the first lines of three top skills repos:

- **Nutlope/hallmark** (52k stars, #2 monthly): the name, then one bold sentence naming the hosts ("A design skill for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and OpenCode that refuses to look AI-generated"), a link line, one centered image, then a "New in v2" list.
- **mattpocock/skills** (321k stars, on daily and weekly): a dark/light `<picture>` logo, a two-sentence pitch, then "Installation (30-second setup)" with one collapsible block per agent host.
- **cloudflare/security-audit-skill** (69k stars, on weekly and monthly): three entries in the whole tree (`LICENSE`, `README.md`, `skills/`). The first screen is a one-line pitch, one line of provenance and a numbered list of what the skill does.

None of them opens with badges, a table of contents or a feature grid.

## What changed since 2026-11-03

- **The monthly list barely moved.** 19 of the 40 repos on an overall list on 2026-11-03 are still on one, and 13 of those 19 are on the monthly list. A repo that makes the monthly list tends to stay there for weeks.
- **14 newcomers**, and 6 of them are agent tooling: addyosmani/agent-skills, vercel-labs/agent-browser, NVIDIA/auto-ontology, The-PR-Agent/pr-agent, homeassistant-ai/ha-mcp and milind-soni/OpenMausBot. The rest are a design system, a physics simulator, a finance app, a web framework, a tutoring agent, a dictation app and two older tools that resurfaced (sherlock and claude-quickstarts).
- **Big companies use the format too.** Anthropic (4 repos), Vercel (2), Cloudflare, NVIDIA, Meta and Google DeepMind all have repos on the lists, and several of those are skills or plugins.

## How to rerun

```bash
cd docs/github-benchmark
GH_TOKEN=$(gh auth token) python3 -I trending_snapshot.py data/trending-YYYY-MM-DD
python3 -I analyze_trending.py data/trending-YYYY-MM-DD
```

The collector writes `lists.json` and `repos.jsonl`. The analyzer prints the appendix tables below as markdown. It skips repos already in `repos.jsonl`, so if a run is interrupted, rerun it and it picks up where it stopped. A full run takes about 25 minutes.
