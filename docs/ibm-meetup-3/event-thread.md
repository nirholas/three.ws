# Official event thread: Community Meetup #3 (IBM Community + X)

The day-of hub for the Three.ws User Group's November 6 meetup. The other drafts in this folder do different jobs: [thread.md](./thread.md) is the long T-7 announcement ("next Friday"), and the [event listing](./event-listing.html) is the full reference. This one is short enough to keep open on a second screen. People use it for questions before the hour, help during it, and screenshots and follow-ups afterwards.

It is written to read correctly whenever someone lands on it: posted the night before, approved by moderation in the morning, or found after the event. It gives dates, never "tomorrow" or "today".

### Get in, in about a minute

1. **Open [three.ws/play](https://three.ws/play)** and click the pinned $THREE world. You can also use the button on [three.ws/event](https://three.ws/event).
2. **You land as an avatar** in about ten seconds. Guests get one automatically. To arrive as yourself, build one beforehand at [three.ws/create](https://three.ws/create) from a selfie or a sentence, and it follows you in.
3. **Walk up to people and talk.** Voice is spatial: you hear the people near you, from the direction they are standing. Text chat works if you would rather stay muted.

**Controls:** WASD to walk (a joystick on phones), **V** to switch avatars, **P** for photo mode, and type `/forge` plus a sentence in chat to drop a generated 3D object into the world.

### Run of show (Pacific)

| Time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 8:00 | Doors open in the plaza. Welcome from IBM Community and the team. |
| 8:06 | Live build: one sentence becomes a rigged 3D agent, then we wear it. |
| 8:14 | Video to motion: a phone clip becomes an animation for any humanoid avatar. |
| 8:20 | Forge into the world: `/forge` in chat, live, for everyone standing there. |
| 8:27 | An agent pays for a tool at the Agent Exchange and leaves a receipt anyone can open. |
| 8:33 | Community showcase: bring your avatars, embeds, MCP integrations and agents. |
| 8:43 | Open Q&A and what ships next. The skeptical questions are the most useful ones. |
| 8:49 | Dance-off on the floor. Step on, then vote with a reaction. |
| 8:55 | Group photo at the totem. The shared shutter fires at 8:58, then fireworks close the hour. |

The world runs this schedule itself. A countdown chip appears in the home town, and each segment announces itself with a banner when it starts. Tap the chip to see the agenda in your own timezone.

### Why it is worth the hour

The first in-world meetup on August 7 was billed as an hour. People stayed for nine, and the world peaked at **3,145 avatars at once**, far more people than a call link could seat. The account of that day is at [three.ws/blog/ibm-user-group-first-in-world-meetup-recap](https://three.ws/blog/ibm-user-group-first-in-world-meetup-recap).

If the room you join is full, you land in a twin instance of the same world. The countdown, banners and fireworks all run off one shared clock, so every instance sees them at the same moment.

### Use this thread

- **Before:** ask anything below. We answer every question before the doors open.
- **During:** if the world will not load or your voice will not connect, reply with your browser and device and someone from the team will answer live.
- **After:** post your photo-mode shots, what you built, and any question we did not get to. The recap goes here too.

See you in the plaza.
