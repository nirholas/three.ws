This is the official thread for the Three.ws User Group's third meetup. Use it for questions before the hour, for help during it, and for your screenshots and follow-ups after it. The event listing has the long version.

The short version: on Friday, November 6, from 8:00 to 9:00 AM Pacific, the group meets inside the $THREE home town at three.ws/play. You attend as a 3D avatar in a browser tab. It is not a video call and it is not a webinar. IBM Community is sponsoring the session.

Keep something Everyone in the world during the hour gets the Meetup Orbit Crown: a free wearable that is never sold and never granted again.

Get in, in about a minute

  1. Open three.ws/play and click the pinned $THREE world. You can also use the button on three.ws/event.
  2. You land as an avatar in about ten seconds. Guests get one automatically. To arrive as yourself, build one beforehand at three.ws/create from a selfie or a sentence, and it follows you in.
  3. Walk up to people and talk. Voice is spatial: you hear the people near you, from the direction they are standing. Text chat works if you would rather stay muted.

Controls: WASD to walk (a joystick on phones), V to switch avatars, P for photo mode, and type /forge plus a sentence in chat to drop a generated 3D object into the world.

The world runs this schedule itself. A countdown chip appears in the home town, and each segment announces itself with a banner when it starts. Tap the chip to see the agenda in your own timezone.

Why it is worth the hour

The first in-world meetup on August 7 was billed as an hour. People stayed for nine, and the world peaked at 3,145 avatars at once, far more people than a call link could seat. The account of that day is at three.ws/blog/ibm-user-group-first-in-world-meetup-recap.

If the room you join is full, you land in a twin instance of the same world. The countdown, banners and fireworks all run off one shared clock, so every instance sees them at the same moment.
