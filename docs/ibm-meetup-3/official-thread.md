### How to get in (about a minute)

1. Open **[three.ws/play](https://three.ws/play)**
2. Click the **$THREE** world pinned at the top
3. You land as a 3D avatar in about ten seconds. Walk up to people and talk. Voice is spatial, so you hear whoever is standing near you, from the direction they're standing.

No download, no install, no account. It runs in a browser tab.

Want to arrive as yourself? Build your avatar first at **[three.ws/create](https://three.ws/create)** from a selfie or one sentence, and it follows you in.

### Controls

**WASD** to walk (joystick on phones) · **V** to switch avatars · **P** for photo mode · type **/forge** plus a sentence in chat to drop a 3D object into the world
