# The Black Bull x three.ws: collab meetup brainstorm and outreach kit

Working doc for approaching Ansem ([@blknoiz06](https://x.com/blknoiz06)) and the Black Bull
community about a joint in-world meetup, listed as an event in the
[Three.ws User Group on IBM Community](https://community.ibm.com/community/user/usergroup?CommunityKey=e71510cc-d953-408f-9a1c-019f5c0a7016).
Facts here were checked on 2026-09-18. Nothing in this file has been sent or posted.

## Read this first: three things that change the pitch

**1. The ticker is `$ANSEM`, not `$BULL`.** The coin is called The Black Bull and trades as
`$ANSEM` on Solana (pump.fun launch, mid-June 2026). Several copycat tokens launched next to it, so
confirm the mint before it goes into any link. The mint the public trackers agree on is
`9cRCn9rGT8V2imeM2BaKs13yhMEais3ruM3rPvTGpump`. Getting the ticker wrong in a first DM is the
fastest way to get ignored, so every draft below says `$ANSEM` and "The Black Bull".

**2. Ansem did not launch the coin.** An anonymous dev deployed it and sent him a large share of
supply. He then embraced it, promoted it, and ran roughly $7M of airdrops to his followers in late
June. So there are two doors, and the second is more likely to open:

- Ansem himself: close to a million followers, an inbox nobody can reach cold. Keep the ask tiny.
- The community leads: the people running the dashboards and sites
  ([ansemdash.com](https://ansemdash.com/), [the-black-bull.com](https://the-black-bull.com/),
  [blackbullsol.com](https://www.blackbullsol.com/)) and the Telegram and X community accounts.
  They can say yes on their own, and a community event that is already happening is a much
  easier thing for Ansem to quote-post than a cold proposal.

**3. Do not call it "an IBM event" or "IBM x $ANSEM".** This is the one way this idea can cost
more than it earns. The user group is a community space three.ws moderates on IBM's platform. It
is not an IBM product and IBM does not co-host or endorse what happens in it (the framing rules
are in [ibm.md](./ibm.md)). If a memecoin community starts posting "IBM partnered with $ANSEM",
that lands on Jessica and on the Business Partner relationship. The accurate line is strong enough
on its own:

> We run an official user group on IBM Community, and our meetups are listed there as group events.

Before the event page goes up, tell Jessica what the collab is and let her decide how visible she
wants to be. On the IBM listing itself, lead with the technology and the two communities, and keep
ticker promotion on X and three.ws/blog, which is where the group's no-crypto-cluster rule already
sends it.

## Event ideas

Ranked by how much they give the Black Bull community a reason to show up. The first four are the
event. The rest are add-ons.

**1. The Stampede: break the record together.** The standing record is 3,145 avatars in the world
at once. One shared goal, one number on screen, one screenshot at the end. "The bulls broke the
three.ws server record" is a post their community writes for us. It also gives Ansem a one-line
reason to post: a number to beat.

**2. A rigged 3D Black Bull, built live, free for every holder.** Mirror past event 2. Open the
event by typing one sentence into the Forge and turning it into a rigged, walking bull in a suit
(humanoid, so it drives the whole animation library). By minute ten everybody in the plaza can
wear it. The community has plenty of 2D memes and no 3D character. We hand them one.

**3. The souvenir: Black Bull Horns.** Every live event grants a free wearable to whoever walks in
during the window, kept forever, never granted again ([how it works](./event-souvenirs.md)). Make
this one a set of black horns in the headwear slot. For a community built on "I was early", a
permanent item that proves you were in the room is the hook. Note: the souvenir grant currently
runs in the `$THREE` home town only, so the main event is hosted there.

**4. Ansem fireside, 15 minutes, spatial voice.** He walks into the plaza as an avatar and talks.
No slides, no stage. People physically crowd around him to hear, which is the clip. This is the
small ask: 15 minutes, from a browser, no download.

**5. After-party in their own world.** The event starts in the `$THREE` town and ends with
everyone walking into the Black Bull world, which exists already from their mint. Builds persist,
so whatever they put up that night is still standing next week. It becomes their place, not ours.

**6. Bulls vs three: King of the Totem.** The hold-the-gold-ring game mode is already in the
world. Two communities, one ring, a scoreboard. Fishing tournament and car races as side
events, both already in the world.

**7. A talking Black Bull for their sites.** The leave-behind. The `<agent-3d>` web component puts
the rigged bull on ansemdash.com or any community site in two lines of HTML, with a voice and a
personality. The event ends, the bull stays on their homepage, and it says "made with three.ws".

**8. The technical segment that earns the IBM listing.** Five minutes on how an agent is stopped
from doing something stupid with money: the Granite Guardian allow / review / block gate from the
2026-09-18 group post. It keeps the event legitimate for the developers in the group and is a
real answer to the question memecoin people actually have about agents: "what stops it rugging
me?"

**9. Photo and clip contest.** The in-world photo mode stamps the event name on every shot. Best
clip from the night wins a cosmetic. Costs nothing, produces a week of posts.

### What we are not asking for

Say this out loud in the pitch, because it is what every other project in his DMs is asking for:
no paid promo, no token swap, no "buy $THREE", no listing fee, no allocation. It is free, it runs
in a browser, and his holders walk away with something permanent.
