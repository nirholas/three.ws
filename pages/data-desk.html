How a purchase works

  1. A brand-new keypair is generated and stored encrypted. It has never appeared on chain.
  2. One funding transaction gives it the exact USDC price of its job plus enough SOL to stay rent-exempt and pay two network fees.
  3. The wallet calls the paid endpoint, receives the 402 challenge, signs one USDC transfer and gets the data back. The settlement is a normal Solana transaction anyone can verify.
  4. The wallet sends any leftover USDC to the treasury, closes its token account so the rent goes back to the funder, and sends every remaining lamport home. It ends at zero and is never used again.

Lane statistics and every wallet's three signatures are public at /api/fresh-workers.
