Solana Seeker Live on the dApp Store

Made for the Seeker, signed by your Seed Vault.

three.ws is approved and live on the Solana dApp Store. Every signature happens inside the Seeker’s Seed Vault, sign-in is a single tap, and holding your Seeker Genesis Token earns a verified badge on every agent you own.

What you get

  • One-tap Seed Vault sign-in

    Sign-In With Solana happens at authorize time, so there is one sheet instead of connect-then-sign. Keys never leave the secure element.

  • Seeker Genesis Token verification

    Prove you hold the soulbound Genesis Token. The check only reads your wallet: no transaction, no signature. Verified owners get a Seeker verified badge on every agent they own.

  • Create from a selfie

    One photo becomes a rigged, animation-ready avatar in about a minute. Give it a voice and a personality and talk to it.

  • Own it on-chain

    Deploy an agent to Solana, held by your wallet. Sell its skills, take tips and trade agents.

  • Marketplace and Explore

    Browse agents from the whole community, orbit them in 3D and open any one to chat.

  • Open and portable

    The app is open source and agents use an open manifest, so nothing locks you in.

Get started on your Seeker

  1. Install from the dApp StoreOpen the Solana dApp Store on your Seeker or Saga and search for three.ws.
  2. Sign in with Seed VaultTap sign in on the home screen and approve once in the Seed Vault sheet.
  3. Verify your SeekerTap Check my wallet. If your Genesis Token is found, the badge appears on your agents.
