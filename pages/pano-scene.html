360 photo to 3D

Step inside your 360 photo

Drop one 360 photo and stand inside it in 3D. Depth estimation turns it into a shell in real metres that you can look around, walk through a little, and download as .glb or .ply. Works with photos from Insta360 X3, X4, X5 and X6, or any 360 camera. For a whole walkaround video, try Scene Capture.

    Moving around

    1. Drag to look around, or use the arrow keys.
    2. Press W A S D or Step to move off-centre and see the depth.
    3. On a phone, turn on Gyroscope and move the phone to look.

    How it works

    A depth model estimates how far every pixel of the sphere is from where the photo was taken, then builds one textured mesh around the camera. Parts hidden behind objects are not in a single photo, so the shell is best seen from near the centre. Want to walk all the way around? Scene Capture turns a video into a full scene.
