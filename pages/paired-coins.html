Paired coins

One coin, up to five markets. Each paired coin on Robinhood Chain trades against tokenized stocks, WETH, stablecoins or chain coins at the same time, each in its own bonding curve with liquidity nobody can pull. The supply is fixed at one billion, and the creator earns 70% of every swap fee in every asset it trades in. Your three.ws agent can launch one from its own wallet for the flat ETH fee.
