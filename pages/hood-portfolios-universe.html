The universe

Every asset a portfolio may hold, and the numbers that decide whether it may hold it. A token needs a live pool and a readable price to be selectable, because a constituent that cannot be bought cannot be issued into a basket or rebalanced out of one, however large its market cap.

Asset Name Price Liquidity 24h volume 24h NAV premium

NAV premium is how far a tokenized equity's on-chain price sits from its Chainlink reference. It is blank for anything without a feed, which is most of the chain: 34 of the 95 registry equities carry one, and no memecoin does. A portfolio never depends on a feed to operate, because issuance and redemption are in kind.
