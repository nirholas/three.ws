Accessibility statement

Built to be used with a keyboard, a screen reader and a zoomed screen

Our commitment

We treat accessibility as part of shipping, not as a later pass. Every page in the core journeys is checked automatically before release, keyboard and focus behaviour is covered by end-to-end tests, and a regression in either fails the build. When we find a barrier we fix it in code and add a test so it cannot come back.

We follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. Wallet and payment surfaces are also held to the Web3 Accessibility Guidelines (W3AG) at Levels A and AA.

Conformance status

three.ws partially conforms with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Partially conforms means that some parts of the content do not fully meet the standard. Of the 56 Level A and AA criteria, 45 are supported, 5 are partially supported and 6 do not apply: the five audio and video criteria, because three.ws has no prerecorded or live media, and 4.1.1 Parsing, which WCAG 2.2 retired.

How we test

  • Automated. axe-core runs through Playwright against 18 page types in dark and light themes at desktop and 400px widths, with every WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA rule. The release gate is zero serious or critical findings.
  • Keyboard only. Each page walked with Tab and Shift+Tab for focus order, visible focus, traps, the skip link and focus hidden under sticky toolbars.
  • Screen reader. Orca 46.1 on Linux with Chromium 145, navigating by heading, landmark, form field, button and graphic.
  • Zoom, spacing and colour. Browser zoom at 200% and 400%, the WCAG text-spacing values, and Windows-style forced colours.

Pages outside the core journeys, such as labs, games, the 3D world at /play and developer tools, have not been evaluated yet and are not covered by this statement.

What helps

  • Light and dark themes from the theme button in the top bar. The choice is remembered.
  • Turn on reduced motion in your operating system to stop auto-rotating 3D previews and animated backgrounds.
  • Focus any 3D view and use the arrow keys to orbit, and + and - to zoom.
  • Agents can be talked to by typing. Voice input is optional.
  • Wallet addresses are read out in groups of four characters instead of as one long string.

Known limitations

These are the gaps we know about, and what to do in the meantime.

Tell us when something gets in your way

Email support@three.ws or use the support page. It helps to include the page address, what you were trying to do, and the browser and assistive technology you use. If you need something on three.ws in another format, ask and we will provide it.
