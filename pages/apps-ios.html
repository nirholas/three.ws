iPhone and iPad Coming to the App Store

The iPhone app is on its way.

The native iOS app is finished and waiting on Apple’s developer enrollment, so it is not on the App Store yet. Nothing about three.ws waits for it: the full platform and real AR Quick Look run in Safari on your iPhone today.

Open three.ws in SafariWorks on iPhone and iPad now Try AR

App Store pending Add three.ws to your home screen from Safari’s share sheet to get a full-screen app today.

What you get

  • Push notifications and badge

    Sales, purchases, follows and everything else you left push on for, with your unread count on the icon. A tap opens the page it is about.

  • Share into three.ws

    Send one to three photos from any app to start the selfie flow, or a .glb from Files to open the upload flow.

  • Quick actions

    Press and hold the icon for Create avatar, Discover, My agents and Notifications.

  • AR with real Quick Look

    Place any model in your room through ARKit, with a genuine on-device USDZ conversion.

  • Agent glance widget

    A home screen widget for your agent, on iOS 17 and later.

  • CarPlay

    A voice-first agent for the car screen, built to CarPlay’s rules. How it works.

On your iPhone today

Safari already runs selfie to avatar, the marketplace, your agents and the Forge, and AR Quick Look works without any app. The native build adds push, sharing in, the widget and CarPlay on top of the same product.

Use it on iPhone now

  1. Open three.ws in SafariGo to three.ws and sign in.
  2. Add it to your home screenTap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen from then on.
  3. Place a model in your roomOpen any avatar or model and tap View in AR. Quick Look anchors it to a real surface.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad
iOS or iPadOS 16 or later; the widget needs 17
AR
A device with an A9 chip or newer
Account
Optional to explore, needed to save
