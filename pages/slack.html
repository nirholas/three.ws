Integration · Slack

Your three.ws agent, inside Slack.

Ask your agent anything in a direct message or a mention, check balances and positions with private /three commands, and approve or cancel the trades it prepares with a button. Nothing moves until you say yes.

Add to Slack How pairing works

Free. You need permission to add apps to your Slack workspace, and a free three.ws account to pair with.

What it does

  • Talk to your agent

    Send the app a direct message, or mention @three.ws in a channel it was invited to. Replies to a mention land in a thread, so channels stay readable. Voice clips and images work too.

  • Private commands

    /three balance, /three portfolio, /three positions and the rest work in any channel. Only you see the answer.

  • Trades wait for your yes

    When your agent prepares a trade, you get a preview with Approve and Cancel buttons. Nothing runs from text alone, and a preview expires after ten minutes.

  • Alerts and a stop button

    Account alerts arrive in your DM. Approve or deny what your spend policy holds with /three approvals, and stop agents at once with /three pause or /three kill.

Set it up

Installing adds the app to your workspace. Pairing connects your own Slack account to your own three.ws account, so each person in a workspace talks to their own agent and nobody else's.

  1. 1

    Add to Slack

    Choose Add to Slack above and approve the app for your workspace. Already installed by a teammate? Skip this: find three.ws under Apps in Slack's sidebar.

  2. 2

    Open the Home tab

    Open the three.ws app in Slack. Its Home tab tells you whether your Slack account is connected and, once it is, which agent answers.

  3. 3

    Pair your account

    Send the app any message. It replies with a pairing code; confirm it on three.ws in Settings, Connections. Or generate a code there first and run /three link <code> in Slack.

Commands

Type them anywhere in Slack. Run /three help for the full list.

/three balance
Your agent wallet's SOL balance.
/three portfolio
Net worth and top holdings.
/three positions
Open positions and their live value.
/three runs
Recent agent runs and trades.
/three approvals
Requests waiting for your yes, with Approve and Deny buttons.
/three agents
List your agents; /three use <agent> picks which one answers.
/three pause
Stop one agent from trading or spending. /three kill stops every agent.
/three link
Pair this Slack account with a code from three.ws. /three unlink disconnects it.

What it can see

  • Only what is sent to it. The app reads direct messages to the app and messages that mention it. It never reads other channels, other people's DMs or your files.
  • Tokens are sealed. Each workspace's bot token is stored encrypted, with the same secret box that protects agent wallets.
  • Uninstall ends it. Removing the app from Slack revokes its token on our side at once, and unpairing with /three unlink disconnects your account.
  • Not sold or shared. Messages are used only to answer you. Full details are in the privacy policy.

Permissions it asks for

  • app_mentions:readSee messages that mention the app, so a mention gets an answer.
  • chat:writePost replies, trade previews and alerts.
  • commandsAdd the /three command.
  • im:historyRead the messages you send the app in its DM.
  • im:readKnow which DM belongs to which person, for pairing.
  • im:writeOpen a DM to send your alerts and approvals.
  • files:readHear a voice clip or read an image you send the app.
  • files:writeReply with a spoken answer when you turn voice replies on.

Questions

Slack says I can't install apps

Your workspace limits who can add apps. Choose Add to Slack anyway and Slack offers to send an install request to your workspace admins; once they approve it, everyone can pair.

The app answers with a pairing code instead of my agent

Your Slack account is not paired yet. Confirm the code in Settings, Connections while signed in to three.ws. Codes expire after a few minutes; send another message for a fresh one.

Can teammates see my balances or trades?

No. Slash command answers are only visible to you, and trade previews and alerts go to your DM with the app. In a channel, the app only answers mentions, in a thread.

Which agent answers me?

Your default agent. Run /three agents to list yours and /three use <agent> to switch. The Home tab always shows the current one.

How do I remove it?

Run /three unlink to disconnect your account, or remove the app in Slack (Settings, Manage apps) to uninstall it for the workspace. Uninstalling revokes its token immediately.
