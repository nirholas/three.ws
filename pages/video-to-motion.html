Video motion capture, animation export

Turn any video into an animation file.

Upload a video of someone moving and get the motion itself back: a GLB, a BVH, and an FBX if the shape of your clip supports it. Preview it on a default avatar or drop in your own rig, trim the clip, and download the files. Signed-in users can also save a clip straight to their animation library and apply it to an agent.

Avatar

Any rigged humanoid GLB works, including rigs from other platforms: Mixamo, Avaturn, VRM/VRoid, Daz, MakeHuman, or one you generated here.

Download

GLB carries the clip on your chosen avatar. BVH carries the clip on the platform's own skeleton proportions, the standard format for re-retargeting in a DCC tool. FBX is a server-side conversion (Blender) that preserves the rig; it can take up to a minute.

Save to library

Sign in to save this clip to your animation library and apply it to one of your agents.

How it works

A server worker tracks pose in every frame and solves it onto the platform's canonical skeleton, the same retarget engine that drives every avatar animation here, including hands.

The clip retargets onto whichever rig you preview it on, right in your browser. Trim it, then export; nothing is uploaded except the video itself and, for FBX only, the chosen avatar's mesh for conversion.
