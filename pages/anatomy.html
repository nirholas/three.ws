Anatomy

Describe a machine. Watch it work.

One line becomes an interactive isometric cutaway: the real moving parts in motion, fire, water and plasma rendered with live shaders, and a guided tour of the physics. Rotate it, cut it open, pull it apart.

Name a machine and what you want to understand. Ctrl Enter to build.

  • Real motionFans spin, pistons ride their cranks and valves open in time, each part moving on its own.
  • Physics you can seeCombustion, coolant, plasma and airflow drawn with WebGL shaders and animated flow paths.
  • Take it apartExplode the assembly, slice a section, x-ray the casing, or follow the tour step by step.
