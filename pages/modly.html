Modly on three.ws

Make 3D on your own graphics card.

Modly is a free, open-source app that turns images into 3D models on your own GPU. three.ws connects to it straight from this page: your image never leaves your machine, there is no queue and no quota, and the finished model comes back here to preview, clean up and save. See every way to use Modly with three.ws.

Looking for Modly.

Modly is not running on this computer. Install it once, start it, and connect again.

  1. 1
    Download Modly for Windows, Linux or Apple Silicon macOS from modly3d.app or its latest GitHub release.
  2. 2
    Open the app. It starts its local server on port 8765 in the background. Keep it open while you work here.
  3. 3
    Add a model from Modly's Models page with Install from GitHub. You can also do this after connecting; this page lists the official ones.

Already running? Check that the address above matches Modly's port, and that your browser lets this site reach apps on your device (Chrome and Edge ask once; Brave has a per-site setting for it).

Your browser blocked the connection to your computer. This site was refused access to apps on your device earlier.

  1. 1
    Click the site settings icon at the left of the address bar.
  2. 2
    Set Local network access for three.ws to Allow.
  3. 3
    Press Retry. Modly only answers on your own machine, so nothing on your network is exposed.

Safari does not let secure pages reach apps on your computer. It treats Modly's local http:// address as insecure content and blocks it, with no setting to allow it.

Open three.ws/modly in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on this Mac, or use the three.ws Forge desktop app. Modly itself works the same either way.
