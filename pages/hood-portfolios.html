Connecting to Robinhood Chain…

Describe a portfolio. Hold it on-chain.

Robinhood Chain is the one place where tokenized equities, crypto majors and memecoins trade against each other on the same chain. Write a sentence and a screen ranks that whole universe against it. What comes back is a real basket with target weights, held in kind by a vault that rebalances on a published schedule.

How it works

Four steps, and only one of them involves a model. The interesting decisions are made before anything touches the chain; what happens on-chain is deliberately the least clever part.

  1. The screen runs

    Your prompt and the chain's holdable universe go to the model. Every token it returns is checked back against that universe, so anything invented is dropped, never repaired into something plausible.

  2. The manifest is committed

    Constituents, weights, schedule, the originating prompt and the universe snapshot are hashed and signed. The hash goes on-chain; the document is content-addressed. It is immutable, so what you bought cannot change underneath you.

  3. The vault holds it in kind

    Deliver every constituent to mint, receive every constituent to redeem. The fund never sells anything to let somebody in or out, so it eats no slippage and needs no price oracle to operate.

  4. It rebalances on schedule

    When the published interval comes due, the move is offered as a descending-price auction any address can fill. There is no function that lets a manager trade the fund's assets. Every portfolio is backtested twice, on its schedule and never rebalanced, so you can see what the schedule actually did rather than assume it helped.

Refining mints a new version, it never edits the old one

Elsewhere, refining an index with a follow-up prompt changes the index you already hold. On-chain that is not a feature, it is the definition of a rug: the manifest is the only thing a holder relies on. So a refinement publishes a new portfolio whose parent pointer is the old one. Holders of the original keep exactly what they bought, and the fork pays its lineage a fixed slice of its fees. It is a budget, not a rule, so forking your own portfolio fifty times cannot pay out more than forking it once.

What this is not

Nothing here is investment advice, and a generated basket is not a recommendation. Tokenized equities on Robinhood Chain are tokenized debt securities issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Ltd and may not be offered, sold or delivered to US persons; this page is display and tooling only. A portfolio holding chain-native tokens can lose all of its value, and a halted equity leg suspends redemption for everyone until its issuer resumes it.
