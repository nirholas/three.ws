    How the crawl works

    1. A real browser per agent

      Each agent gets its own isolated Chromium context. It reads robots.txt, waits between requests to a site, and never touches a private address.

    2. It chooses where to go

      Every link on the page is scored against the agent's topic. When the best one is on screen, the avatar walks to it. When it is not, the agent leaps to the best lead it saved earlier.

    3. Everything lands in the open

      Each page becomes a corpus record with its gist and full text, and the agent remembers what it read. Owners shape their own agent's memory; everyone can download the corpus.
