Android APK available

Your 3D agents, native on Android.

A 4 MB app that opens three.ws full-screen with no browser bar. Make an avatar from a selfie, talk to your agents, place them in your room in AR, and sign with the wallet already on your phone.

Download the APKVersion 1.0.0, 4 MB Open in your browser

Google Play soon The Play Store listing is being prepared. The APK is the same signed build that ships on the Solana dApp Store (package ws.three.app).

What you get

  • Full-screen, no browser chrome

    A Trusted Web Activity opens three.ws in Chrome’s rendering engine, so 3D runs at full speed and links to three.ws open inside the app.

  • Sign with your own wallet

    Mobile Wallet Adapter connects the Solana wallet on your phone. Sign-in is one tap and the session survives Android closing the app.

  • Selfie to avatar

    Take a photo and get a rigged 3D avatar in about a minute, or describe a character in text.

  • AR in your room

    View in AR places any avatar or model on a real surface through your camera.

  • Agent glance widget, next release

    Version 1.1.0 adds a home screen widget that shows your agent and its latest status. Link it from your account and revoke it any time. How the widget works.

  • Shortcuts and offline

    Long-press the icon for Create, Discover and My agents. A designed offline screen reloads itself when the network returns.

Install it

  1. Download the APKTap Download the APK on your phone. Android may warn that APKs can harm your device; that is the standard prompt for any app outside a store.
  2. Allow the installWhen asked, allow your browser or Files app to install apps, then open the downloaded file and tap Install.
  3. Open three.ws and sign inConnect your wallet or sign in with your account. Your agents and models are already there.

Requirements

Android
Android 6.0 or later with Chrome installed and up to date
Camera
Only for selfie capture and AR; you can upload a photo instead
Wallet
Optional. Any Mobile Wallet Adapter wallet, such as Seed Vault on a Seeker

On a Solana Seeker or Saga? See the Seeker page. The packaging and release notes live in the source repository.
