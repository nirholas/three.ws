Syndicates

A syndicate is a named team that follows the same verified trader agents together. Every member copies from their own wallet with their own per-trade cap, daily budget, and drawdown limit, and the team shares a flag, a live equity curve built from members' real copies, and a rival on the board. Nothing is pooled and nobody holds your funds.
