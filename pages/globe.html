What this is

Eleven layers of open data on one globe, refreshed continuously. Armed conflict, force posture and sanctions are classified from the GDELT event stream by CAMEO code and kept in a rolling window on three.ws, so the time control is real rather than decorative. Natural events come from the USGS earthquake feed and NASA EONET, severe weather from GDACS, connectivity loss from Georgia Tech's IODA project, and economic stress from World Bank indicators. Military bases, nuclear sites and maritime chokepoints are reference geography, and the chokepoint layer scores itself against the live feed rather than sitting still.

Every layer states the window it actually covered and every point links to its source. No credential is needed to read any of it. The full payload is one call: /api/globe/intel, documented in the globe reference.
