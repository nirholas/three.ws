Sign review

three.ws avatars sign American Sign Language today. Any other sign language is added one sign at a time, and only fluent signers decide whether a sign is right. Watch each sign on the avatar, slow it down, flip the dominant hand, then approve it, reject it, or say what needs to change.

A sign never goes live without the required approvals. Each sign needs approvals from distinct reviewers on its exact current form. Editing a sign resets it to unreviewed, and a single rejection holds it back.
