Apps/Web desktop

Web desktop Live

A desktop that lives at a web address.

Open three.ws and it boots like a computer, then lets you walk in as your own avatar and sit down at your desk. Every part of the platform is a window: make agents, forge 3D models, trade, browse the marketplace.

Open the desktopthree.ws, no install Classic homepage

Works in current Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox. Chrome and Edge also offer to install it as an app from the address bar.

What you get

  • Boots with real checks

    The boot log is live: your GPU, the site catalog, your account, your avatar and the $THREE feed are each checked as it starts, and a failed check says so.

  • Walk in as your avatar

    A 3D campus with the Exchange, Launchpad, Forge, Gallery, Agent Plaza and Marketplace. Sit at your desk and the desktop opens on the monitor.

  • Windows on real pages

    Taskbar, Start menu, widgets and snappable windows frame the real three.ws pages. Icons snap to a grid and All Apps lists everything.

  • Yours, saved in the browser

    Settings keeps your profile, accent, wallpaper and icon size, and your session survives a reload.

  • Install it

    Add it to your home screen or desktop as a progressive web app and it opens in its own window.

  • Reach it from any phone

    The same address runs on a phone browser, with AR placement and the Pocket Console built in.

Get started

  1. Open three.wsGo to three.ws. The boot screen runs, then the lock screen greets you.
  2. Choose how to enterPick Walk in for the campus or Desktop to go straight to the windows.
  3. Sign in when you want to keep thingsBrowsing is open. Sign in to save agents, models and your gem balance to your account.

Requirements

Browser
Current Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox with WebGL 2
Graphics
Any GPU from the last several years; the campus is optional
Account
Optional to explore, needed to save
